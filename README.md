A make.fun entry for the **Pigeon Pitch, One Line** bounty: write the one-line elevator pitch a city pigeon gives investors for its startup, at most 200 characters, in English.

## The entry

> We're Uber for breadcrumbs: I deliver crumbs citywide in under a minute, take 30% off the top, and our drivers poop on the competition — literally, from 40 feet up.

(164 characters. Uber for breadcrumbs, a 30% platform cut paid in crumbs, and drivers who poop on the competition — literally, from 40 feet up.)

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
```

This writes the single line to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`), with in-code gate checks: single line, non-blank, 1–200 characters.
